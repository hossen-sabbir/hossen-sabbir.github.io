Research

Multimodal and Foundation Models

I am interested in large language models and vision-language models, with emphasis on language understanding, reasoning, efficient adaptation, and multimodal learning.

Efficient and Resource-Constrained AI

This direction focuses on efficient architectures, model compression, optimization, and methods that make modern AI practical under limited compute and data.

Multilingual and Low-Resource NLP

I work on NLP problems involving Bangla and other resource-constrained languages, including fake news detection, sentiment analysis, and generative-AI text analysis.

Computer Vision and Low-Resource Healthcare

My computer-vision work includes deepfake detection and AI applications for low-resource healthcare environments.
