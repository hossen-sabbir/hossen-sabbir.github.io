About Me

I am a recent Computer Science graduate from Bangladesh University with a strong foundation in Machine Learning, and I am currently working as a Research Assistant at at National Institute of Textile Engineering and Research . My research experience primarily focuses on Natural Language Processing and Computer Vision, through which I have developed both analytical and creative approaches to problem-solving. These experiences have also led me to develop a strong interest in Large Language Models and Vision-Language Models, particularly in exploring efficient approaches for low-resource and resource-constrained settings.

My long-term goal is to build computational systems with a broader and more coherent understanding, where vision models, language models, and generative frameworks work in concert rather than in isolation. I aim to develop techniques that not only interpret images and text but also ground their reasoning in real-world structure across diverse application domains, with an emphasis on efficiency, resource constraints, and low-resource settings. I am committed to conducting rigorous, forward-looking research that contributes meaningfully to this direction. For more information about my research background, ongoing work, and academic experience related to these interests, please take a look at my background, research and publications sections.

If you are interested in research collaboration, have questions about my work, or would like to discuss a potential research opportunity, please feel free to reach out to me at sabbir.hossen@bu.edu.bd . You can also connect with me through my social profiles to follow my latest research activities, publications, projects, and other academic updates.
